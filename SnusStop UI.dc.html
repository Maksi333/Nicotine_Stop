SnusStop — hi-fi UI

Material 3 Android (412px) with a playful gamified layer per the handoff. Type: Nunito. Palette: mint green primary, coral for Craving SOS, gold for XP/streaks, lavender for calm/breathe. Sample persona: Mikkel, quit 12 days ago, 15 pouches/day, 45 kr per 20-pouch can — counters tick live and are driven by the Tweaks panel (days quit, pouches/day, can price). Mascot "Puff" is a simple bubble face placeholder — swap for real brand art later. Sections: onboarding · dashboards · goals, health & journey · SOS & slips · widgets · settings & notifications.

3Dark mode for the other tabs — pairs with Home 1j. Both themes ship; dark follows the system setting.
